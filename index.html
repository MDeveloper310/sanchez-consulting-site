Good to Know

Frequently asked questions

Which appointment should I book?

Choose the appointment that matches your return. Not sure which one fits? Text (415) 200-7148 and Telma will schedule you.

Do married couples need one appointment or two?

Each appointment covers one tax return. Married filing jointly is one return for both spouses, so one appointment. Two single people filing separately need two appointments.

Is a deposit required, and how is it used?

Yes. A non-refundable deposit is taken through the online scheduler when you book, and it is applied toward your tax preparation fee. Consultations and Quick Tax Questions are paid in full at booking.

What is the cancellation and rescheduling policy?

Please give at least 24 hours’ notice to reschedule. Appointments canceled or rescheduled with less than 24 hours’ notice, or missed without notice, forfeit the deposit. For consultations, payment may be transferred one time to a new appointment with 24 hours’ notice.

Where is the office?

The office is in San Francisco. The address is provided after your appointment is confirmed — please text (415) 200-7148 before your appointment to receive location details.

Can I meet remotely?

Tax Strategy Consultations and Quick Tax Questions can be held by phone, Zoom, FaceTime, or in person in San Francisco — you choose when you book.

What should self-employed and business clients bring?

Annual totals by category for income and business expenses, rather than individual receipts, plus vehicle mileage and home-office figures if they apply. Keep your receipts and records for your own files.

How far back can a return be amended?

Amendments can be prepared for up to three prior tax years.
